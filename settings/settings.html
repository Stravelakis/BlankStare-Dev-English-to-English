Paste your Gemini key

No card, no billing. Just a Google account. On the free tier Google may read what you send to improve its AI, so don’t select private information.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey ↗ and sign in with Google.
  2. Click Create API key.
  3. Copy the key and paste it below.

Paste your Groq key

No card, no billing. Just an email address.

  1. Open console.groq.com/keys ↗ and make a free account.
  2. Click Create API Key and give it any name.
  3. Copy the key (it starts with gsk_) and paste it below.

    Three steps

    1. Select confusing text on any page: an error, a README, an install guide.
    2. Right-click and choose Explain with BlankStare. The panel opens with the answer.
    3. Do it. Commands come in copy boxes, steps are numbered. Ask a follow-up if you are stuck.

    Under each answer

    🧭 What do I need first?Accounts, programs and files to have ready.
    ✅ Do it with meThe steps as a checklist you tick off.
    ↓ More detailGoes further into the same thing.
    ↺ Different wordingSame meaning, new words.
    📄 Full pageA short summary of the page you are on.
    💬 Ask a follow-upType any question about the answer.
    📸 Paste a screenshotPress Ctrl+V in the panel.
    🔊 Read aloudHear the answer read aloud.

    Good to know

    • BlankStare uses free AI. It picks a working model every day by itself.
    • It can be wrong. When a step could delete or break something, it says so. Read warnings before you run commands.
    • The text you select is sent to the AI you use (OVHcloud, Google or Groq). Don’t select passwords or private data.

    BlankStare is for people who learn by doing: you found install steps, an error message or a README, and you need to know what it means and what to type. It explains it in plain English or Greek, keeps the exact commands, and walks you through.
