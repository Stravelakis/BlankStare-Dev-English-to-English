## Like I'm five

Some grown-ups write in a secret language full of strange words. Programmers do this a lot.

BlankStare is a helper that lives at the side of your web browser. When you see words you don't understand, you colour them in with your mouse, press a little button, and the helper says the same thing again in easy words.

- If it's still too hard, you can ask it to make it even easier.
- If you want to know more, you can ask it to say more.
- It can speak English or Greek, and it can read the words out loud to you.

To work, it borrows a very clever computer from a company called Groq. They let you use it for free. You just need your own ticket, which is called a key.

It only reads the words you colour in, unless you ask it to read the whole page.
