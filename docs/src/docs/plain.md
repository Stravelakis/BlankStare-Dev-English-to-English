## In plain English

BlankStare is a free add-on for Google Chrome. It opens a panel at the side of your browser. When you read something written for programmers, like an error message, a setup guide or a page of documentation, you select it, and the panel rewrites it in plain English or Greek.

It does not add notes on top of the original. It gives you a version you can read **instead of** the original, and you can follow it step by step.

### What you need

- Google Chrome (or Edge, Brave, or another browser built on Chrome).
- Nothing else. It works with no account and no key.
- If you want faster or better answers: a free key from Google Gemini or Groq. Each takes about three minutes and needs no card.

### How you use it

1. Open the panel with the toolbar button or **Alt + Shift + B**.
2. Select the confusing text on any web page.
3. Right-click and choose **Explain with BlankStare**, or press the small brass button that appears.
4. Read the answer as it appears. Commands come in boxes with a **Copy** button, and steps are numbered.

From there you can:

- **What do I need first?** Lists the accounts, programs and files to have ready.
- **Do it with me.** Turns the steps into a checklist you tick off as you go.
- **More detail**, or **Different wording**.
- **Change the reading level**, from "explain it to a child" up to "I build things with AI tools", and ask again.
- **Ask a follow-up**, or tap a ready-made question like "Is this safe to do?".
- **Paste a screenshot** with **Ctrl + V** and have it explained.
- **Hear it read aloud**, in English or Greek.
- **Summarise the whole page**, see the **term glossary**, or look through your **last 20 answers**.

### Which AI answers

BlankStare uses free AI services and checks every day which free models are working. It uses the best one and moves to the next by itself if one is busy or gone. Under each answer it shows which model wrote it.

- **No key:** OVHcloud, in Europe. Free, but slower when it is busy.
- **Free Google Gemini key:** the best answers, and they can be read aloud in Gemini's own voice.
- **Free Groq key:** the fastest answers.

### Your privacy

- The text you select (or a screenshot you paste) goes to the AI that answers: OVHcloud, Google or Groq. If you ask for a summary, the whole page goes.
- On Google's free tier, Google says it may use what you send to improve its AI, and people at Google may read it. Don't select private information.
- Once a day BlankStare downloads a public list of free models from The Nein. Nothing about you is sent.
- Your keys and settings stay in your own browser.
- You can switch BlankStare off completely for any website you choose.

### Cost

Nothing. Every option is free.

### It can be wrong

It is an AI. When a step could delete or break something, it says so, and it says when it is not sure. Read warnings before you run a command.
