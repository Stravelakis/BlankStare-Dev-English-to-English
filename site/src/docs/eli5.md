## What is it?

Grown-up computer people write in a secret language. BlankStare is a friendly
helper that sits next to your web page and says, "Here is what that means,"
in normal words.

## How do I use it?

You colour in the confusing words with your mouse. BlankStare explains them.
You can even ask it to talk to you, in English or in Greek.

## Does it cost money?

No. It borrows a free robot brain. You just need a free password for that
robot, called a key, and you keep it yourself.

## Is it always right?

Not always. It is a robot. If something sounds odd, the helper gives you
links to proper books about it, so you can check.
