Handling stream errors

When a streaming response is interrupted, the reader throws. Wrap the read loop so the partial response is preserved rather than discarded, and surface the interruption to the caller.

A dropped connection mid-stream is indistinguishable from a completed one unless you track whether the terminating sentinel arrived. Treat a missing sentinel as an error even when the socket closed cleanly.
