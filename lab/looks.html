Side panel — motion that helps, never distracts

The panel stays open all day beside your work, so nothing moves while you read. Motion only marks "working" and "done". Press Play to stream a real answer.

1 · Decode + glowing edgeMy pick (with 2)
  • While the answer streams, a brass light runs around the card's edge.
  • The first sentence decodes from symbols; the rest fades in word by word.
  • Stops completely once done.
2 · Prompt bar with sparksMy pick (with 1)
  • The follow-up box lifts and glows when you click in; the send button morphs.
  • Sending throws a small burst of sparks.
  • Suggestion chips for people who don't know what to ask.
3 · Quiet scannerCalmest
  • Only a thin brass line sweeping under the header while working.
  • Words fade in softly. No borders, no sparks.
  • For people who want almost nothing moving.
